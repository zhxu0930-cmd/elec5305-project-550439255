## Comparison of Additive and FM Synthesis for Piano and Flute Tone Modelling

This project compares additive synthesis and frequency modulation (FM) synthesis for modelling piano and flute tones.

### Research Question

How effectively can additive synthesis and FM synthesis reproduce the spectral and temporal characteristics of piano and flute tones, and how do the two methods differ in modelling accuracy and parameter complexity?

### Target Instruments

- Piano
- Flute

### Target Notes

- C4
- E4
- A4

### Methods

- Additive synthesis
- Frequency modulation (FM) synthesis
- Spectral and temporal analysis
- Log-spectral distance (LSD)
- Time-varying amplitude envelope

### Platform

- MATLAB
