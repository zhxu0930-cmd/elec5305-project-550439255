ELEC5305 audio signal processing project comparing additive and FM synthesis for modelling piano and flute tones.

## Comparison of Additive and FM Synthesis for Piano and Flute Tone Modelling

This project investigates and compares additive synthesis and frequency modulation (FM) synthesis for modelling piano and flute tones.

The project will analyse the spectral and temporal characteristics of real musical instrument recordings and evaluate how effectively the two synthesis methods can reproduce these characteristics.

### Target Instruments
- Piano
- Flute

### Target Notes
- C4
- E4
- A4

### Methods
- Additive Synthesis
- Frequency Modulation (FM) Synthesis

### Platform
- MATLAB
